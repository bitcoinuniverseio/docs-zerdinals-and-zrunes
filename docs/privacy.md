# Privacy on Zcash, honestly

Zcash is known for shielded privacy. Zerdinals and ZRunes do not use the shielded pools.

1. Zerdinals and ZRunes use transparent Zcash transactions. Addresses, assets, content, and activity are publicly visible.
2. Shielding an asset-bearing output does not make the asset private. It ends protocol tracking: the artifact becomes untrackable or the balance is burned, permanently.
3. Nothing in this product should be read as making your on-chain activity private.

If you need financial privacy on Zcash, use shielded ZEC through a shielded wallet. Keep that activity separate from your artifact activity.
