# Code of conduct

This project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) exist to make the
documentation better. Participation is open to anyone who acts accordingly.

## Expectations

- Be direct about the work and respectful to the people doing it.
- Argue with evidence; the chain and the deployed product settle most
  disagreements here.
- Assume competence and good intent in questions and reviews.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope; this repository documents one
  product.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content,
doxxing, and sustained disruption. Also unacceptable in this project
specifically: promoting scams, fabricating chain facts, or soliciting
investment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and block
contributors who violate this document. Report conduct problems through the
private vulnerability reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if
they are sensitive, or by contacting the maintainers on any open issue if
they are not.
