# Support

**Product questions** (what something means, how to do something): start
with the [FAQ](src/content/docs/help/faq.md) and
[Known limitations](src/content/docs/help/known-limitations.md). If the
documentation does not answer you, that is a documentation bug; please
[open an issue](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes/issues/new/choose)
saying what you were trying to learn.

**Availability questions** (is something down, why is a flow gated): the
[status page](src/content/docs/start/status.mdx) is the authority, and the
live endpoints it names let you check yourself.

**Lost or stuck assets**: read
[Interruptions and recovery](src/content/docs/own/recovery.md) and
[Protect asset-bearing outputs](src/content/docs/own/protect.md) first;
they cover every recoverable situation. Chain-level outcomes (a burned
balance, an untrackable artifact) cannot be reversed by support, and nobody
who claims otherwise is telling the truth.

**Security problems**: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), privately.
