## What you are looking at

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Read from the chain, by us" icon="approve-check">
    Every figure on zrunes.io comes from a Zcash node and an indexer the same
    team operates. Nothing is quoted from a third party and nothing is
    estimated. Where the chain has not been read in full, the product says so
    instead of showing an empty catalogue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The shielded pool is a state, not a gap" icon="seti:lock">
    An artifact spent into a shielded pool provably exists and cannot be
    observed. The product records that as a terminal state and never guesses a
    shielded owner. [How that works](/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes/understand/transparent-and-shielded/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Two ZRC-20 readings, both named" icon="seti:json">
    Two independent rule sets read the same blocks and disagree about some
    tokens. The product computes both, shows both, and marks every figure with
    the reading it came from. [Why](/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes/understand/zrc-20/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Honest availability" icon="information">
    Creating and transferring require wallet signing that is still gated;
    the product and these docs say so plainly rather than showing controls
    that cannot finish. [Current status](/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes/start/status/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Safety in one paragraph

An inscription or a ZRune balance lives on one specific transparent output,
and that output also holds a small amount of ZEC. A wallet that does not know
about inscriptions can spend it as a fee or as change, and the asset goes with
it. Keep artifact addresses separate from spending addresses, and check what
an output carries before you spend it.
[The sixty-second version](/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes/start/safety/).
